01 // Origin & Philosophy

Bridging Physical Hardware & High-Level Intelligence

My engineering trajectory did not begin behind an abstraction barrier. It started with stripped wires, microcontrollers, and soldering irons in Nepal — designing autonomous mobile rovers, IoT accident-reporting telemetry modules, and disaster-response drones designed to traverse steep Himalayan topographies to deliver emergency pharmaceutical payloads.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I translated rapid hardware prototyping into clinical utility by designing and building a wireless Hospital Helpline Robot to facilitate contactless patient monitoring and sanitation rounds in infectious wards, shielding front-line medical staff.

Transitioning to Australia, I completed my Bachelor of Information Technology at Asia Pacific International College (APIC), earning the honor of Valedictorian & Graduation Representative Speaker alongside the Dean’s Merit List. On the national innovation stage, my team won the GovHack 2023 Generative AI National Championship for InflunzAI, synthesizing national open data into predictive disease modeling.

Today, as an R&D engineer and founder at Babal Builders, I engineer automation, robotics, and non-destructive moisture remediation technologies (like the Thermal Water Ejector) for modern construction and critical infrastructure.

Core Engineering Tenets

01. First-Principles Mechanics Break down problems to physical realities before choosing software frameworks or microchips.
02. Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Validate digital simulations against real sensors, thermal stresses, and noisy RF environments.
03. Mission-Critical Reliability Build fail-safes into emergency medical aerial systems, hospital rovers, and construction IoT.
